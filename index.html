Prepare a factual account of what happened, when, and where. Avoid posting private contact details, medical records, identifying information about minors, or unverified accusations about private individuals.

Corrections and fairness

We welcome documentary evidence, factual corrections, and responses from agencies and people mentioned in published material. Content will be evaluated for relevance, sourcing, privacy, and potential harm before publication.
